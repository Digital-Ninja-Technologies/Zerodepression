How your privacy is protected
  • There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics on this page. Your browser is given a random anonymous ID so the chat knows which conversation is yours. It disappears when you close this tab.
  • We store your messages and nickname so a counsellor can reply. Volunteer counsellors can read them, and nobody else.
  • The chat runs on Google Firebase. As with any website, Google can see technical details such as your internet address. We can't (except during a voice call, see below), and we never see your name, email, phone number or location unless you choose to submit contact details.
  • Voice calls are optional and only happen if you start or accept one. They connect your browser directly to the counsellor's, so for the length of the call the counsellor's browser can technically see your internet address (which shows your network and rough area, not your name). Calls are not recorded. If you'd rather stay completely anonymous, keep to text.
  • If you choose to submit contact details, only the counsellor who accepts your request can see your phone number or email. Your details are deleted once they have followed up.
  • When you start a chat or send your details, our website lets online counsellors know that someone is waiting. That alert never includes your nickname, your messages or your details.
  • Anyone using this device can see this page, so use Quick exit (or press Esc twice) if you need to leave fast, and Clear this conversation when you're done.

While you wait
  • Say what's on your mind. You can type your first message now. The counsellor will see it as soon as they join, so you won't have to start from scratch.
  • Slow your breathing. Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds, then out slowly for 6. Repeat a few times.
  • Ground yourself. Look around and name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste.
  • Get comfortable. Sit somewhere you feel safe, drop your shoulders and have a sip of water.
  • Rather not wait here? Use Submit contact details above. The first counsellor to accept your request will follow up with you.
  • Keep this page open. Your chat is private to this tab, so closing it ends the wait.
You matter, and you don't have to face this alone. If you might act on thoughts of ending your life, please call 112 now or go to your nearest hospital. You can also call us free on 0800 1100 2200. A counsellor will keep talking with you here.
