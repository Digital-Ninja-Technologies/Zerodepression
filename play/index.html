Games that are kind to your mind

Four free games made in Nigeria by ZeroDepression. Play for a few minutes and leave feeling a little lighter. No sign-up, no downloads, no ads.

Start with Danfo Breathe

Choose a game

  • Danfo Breathe

    Drive a danfo across Lagos at sunset. Hold to breathe in, let go to breathe out. Calm breathing keeps the bus moving and picks up passengers.

    2 minutesBreathing

    Play Danfo Breathe

  • Ọjà Èrò: The Thought Market

    Customers bring heavy thoughts to your stall. Spot the thinking trap, then trade it for a lighter, truer thought.

    5 minutesThinking

    Open the stall

  • Ìrọ̀rùn Garden

    Grow a garden with small acts of self-care, like a glass of water, a short walk or a text to a friend. Miss a day and your garden simply waits for you.

    A few minutes a daySelf-care

    Visit your garden

  • Lantern Walk

    Walk through a quiet night and read lanterns left by people across Nigeria. Then light one with a kind message for a stranger.

    3 minutesConnection

    Start the walk

Games help. People help more.

These games are a gentle break, not a replacement for support. If things feel heavy, talk to a ZeroDepression counsellor. It's free, and you don't need to give your name.
