Meet ZeroDepression

A safe place to say what you've been holding in

Created out of a desire to find a solution to rising depression among young people, ZeroDepression connects you with counsellors who provide guidance for free.

How it works

Three simple steps to feeling heard

  1. Reach out

    Call our toll-free line, start an anonymous chat, or send a message. You never have to share your name.

  2. Talk to a counsellor

    Speak openly with someone who listens with an open mind and no bias.

  3. Keep going, together

    Counsellors follow up with you along your journey so you're never left to work it out alone.

Our partners

People and organisations partnering with us, and those who have partnered with us.

Partner · Lagos, Nigeria

Arogi Trauma Care Foundation

Arogi Trauma Care Foundation (ATCF) makes free counselling and therapy accessible to people dealing with trauma. It offers free counselling through a toll-free hotline and in-person sessions, and raises mental health awareness through talks on radio, television, newspapers and social media.

We have worked side by side on outreaches such as the Mental Health Walk at the Federal University of Technology, Owerri.
